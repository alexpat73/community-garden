A The nav is on every page; the current page is marked (tan on the real site).

Home

  1. Intro: who the site is for and what it does.
  2. "Right now": the month, its Minecraft mini-game (September pumpkin, October carrot) and the live 7-day forecast with frost warnings.
  3. 12 flip cards, one per month. Hovering flips a card (weather, frost risk, how much to sow, plant and harvest); clicking opens the month. This month is outlined.
  4. QR code that opens the mobile app on a phone.
  5. FAQ for first-time gardeners: the questions fold open one at a time.

One month

  1. Previous and next month, like the previous/next album on the original design.
  2. The month's card.
  3. Jump buttons to the Sow, Plant and Harvest sections.
  4. Typical German weather: day, night, rain, daylight, frost risk.
  5. Jobs for the month.
  6. What to start indoors, sow outdoors, plant out and harvest, with "easy" tags for beginners.
  7. Your own notes from this month in earlier years.

All plants

  1. Intro and the key: I = start indoors, S = sow outdoors, P = plant out, H = harvest.
  2. The whole year as a table, one row per plant; this month's column is highlighted.
  3. A profile for each of the 31 plants. Clicking a plant's name jumps to it, framed by the animated dashed border (dashed here).

My notes

  1. "All fields are required", said before typing (error prevention).
  2. The same look for every field; the note has a 1000-character counter.
  3. A plain-language message under each empty field.
  4. Save, then download the notes or load them from a file, to hand over to the next plot holder.
  5. The diary, newest first, each with a Delete button.

Find a garden

  1. Prototype banner and "Reset prototype data".
  2. Your groups, shown only once you're in one.
  3. Search by name, neighbourhood or postcode; district and type filters; Near me.
  4. Results, 30 at a time. Click one to open the garden.
  5. The street map loads only after "Show map" (privacy).

A garden, as a visitor

  1. Details: type, neighbourhood, district, website, map link.
  2. The group: "run by its members, not an official page", description, members.
  3. Public notes anyone can read.
  4. How many members-only notes are hidden.
  5. Ask to join: click it to see the member view (the pretend admin accepts).

A garden, as a member

  1. The confirmation, next to where you are.
  2. New note: text, "Members only" or "Public", an optional photo (iPhone HEIC works).
  3. All notes, each marked Members only or Public.
  4. Members and what they grow.
  5. Your profile in the group; Leave this group takes you back to the visitor view.

B Three tabs, each with an icon and a label; the current one is marked (tan in the app).

Welcome

  1. Logo and the tagline.
  2. Just a name and an optional district: no email or password, since this is a prototype.
  3. Get started opens Home.

Home

  1. "Welcome, {name}", the tagline and which month it is.
  2. The week's weather as day cards you swipe, with a frost warning.
  3. Update garden and Photo note: both open the Update sheet (Photo note opens the camera).
  4. This month's jobs to tick off.
  5. What's in season (tap a plant) and the 12 months, small.
  6. Updates from your garden groups.
  7. Your latest notes.
  8. The month's mini-game.
  9. Suggestion box.

Explore

  1. One search box for district, neighbourhood or postcode, and Near me.
  2. Filters: All, Community, Allotment, With a group.
  3. The street map, loaded after one tap (or always, if allowed in Settings).
  4. Results in a pull-up panel; tap one to open the garden's sheet.

Settings

  1. Profile: photo, name, district.
  2. Permissions: location, map, sounds, and the default for new group notes.
  3. The same FAQ as the desktop site.
  4. How to install the app (it works offline).
  5. Download notes, reset the prototype.
  6. Log out (keeps your data; try it) and Delete profile (the only red button).

Update garden (sheet)

  1. What did you do: date, what happened, plant, note. Saved to My notes.
  2. Optional photo straight from the camera; location data is removed.
  3. What you're growing, also shown on your group cards.

A garden (sheet)

  1. Type, neighbourhood and distance.
  2. Public notes.
  3. How many members-only notes are hidden.
  4. Ask to join; once accepted, the private notes appear here.
