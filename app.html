        Install the app

        Add it to your home screen: it opens full screen and works without signal on your plot.

        • iPhone: tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
        • Android: tap the menu, then "Install app".

        Your data

        Everything is saved only on this phone.

        Account

        Logging out keeps your notes and groups on this phone.

        Deleting erases your name, notes, garden groups, jobs, suggestions and settings from this phone. Download your notes first if you want to keep them.
